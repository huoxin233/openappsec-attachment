What install.sh does (and extra flags: --dry-run, --list)
  1. Detects your OS, architecture, and installed nginx package version.
  2. Downloads the matching .tar.gz and verifies its SHA-256 against index.json before unpacking.
  3. Backs up existing attachment .so files and /etc/nginx/nginx.conf (auto-rollback on error).
  4. Installs ngx_cp_attachment_module.so and helper libraries, adds load_module if missing, runs nginx -t, and reloads NGINX.
